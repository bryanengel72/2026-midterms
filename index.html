Polls show where a race stood when voters were surveyed. They are not predictions, and a lead within the margin of error is effectively a tie.

Data: polls, aggregates and ratings are read from each race's article on English Wikipedia and used under CC BY-SA 4.0. Wikipedia editors compile them from the original pollsters and forecasters; follow the article links to check any number at its source.

Betting odds are the implied chance of winning from prices on Polymarket, a prediction market. They reflect what bettors are willing to pay, not what voters told pollsters, and thinly traded markets can swing on a few bets.
